Privacy Policy

App name: Color Tap

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Color Tap is a simple offline arcade game. We respect your privacy and designed the app to collect as little information as possible.

Advertising

Color Tap uses Google AdMob to show occasional short video ads. After every 6 completed games, you may be shown an interstitial video ad before starting the next game.

AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address, and ad interaction data to deliver and measure ads. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses data in AdMob.

Information we collect

Color Tap itself does not collect, store, or share personal information directly.

  • No account is required
  • No first-party analytics are used
  • Gameplay works offline; ads require an internet connection when shown
  • No location, contacts, photos, or microphone access is requested

Data stored on your device

Your best score and ad frequency counter are stored locally on your device using Android shared preferences. This data is not sent to our servers.

Children

Color Tap is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Third-party services

Color Tap uses Google AdMob for advertising. If you install the app from Google Play, Google may also process information according to its own privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
