# Data safety form — Color Tap

Use these answers in Play Console → **App content → Data safety**.

## Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?

**No** — for data collected directly by Color Tap.

However, you must disclose that **third-party advertising (Google AdMob)** may collect data. In Play Console, when asked about third-party data collection related to ads, answer accordingly.

## Data stored locally on device

- **High score** — stored in app preferences, not transmitted
- **Games completed counter** — used for ad frequency, stored locally

## Third-party SDK: Google AdMob

AdMob may automatically collect for ad delivery and measurement:

- Device or other IDs (Advertising ID)
- IP address
- Ad interaction data
- Diagnostic data

Refer to [Google's AdMob data disclosure](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543) when completing the form. Google provides a guided flow for apps using AdMob.

## Security practices

- Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS for ad requests)
- Users can request data deletion: not applicable (no account, no server-side user data)
- Data is not sold

## Ads declaration

- **Does the app contain ads?** Yes
- **Are ads from a certified ad network?** Yes (Google AdMob)
