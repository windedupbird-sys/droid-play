Color Tap is a fast, simple arcade game built for quick play sessions.

Colored circles appear on screen and begin fading away. Tap them before they disappear to score points. Faster taps earn more points. Miss too many circles and it is game over.

Features:
• 3 Game Modes: Classic Survival, 60s Time Attack, and Relaxing Zen Mode
• Procedural synthesizer sound effects & tactile Android haptic feedback
• Multiplier combos and explosive Fever Mode
• Special target varieties: Golden bonuses, Heart heals, Freeze slowdowns, moving targets, shielded circles, and Bomb hazards
• Unlockable visual themes: Classic Neon, Cyberpunk, Pastel Dream, Sunset Glow, and Retro Arcade
• In-game Achievements system & Local Top 5 Leaderboards per mode
• Pause & resume support with detailed post-game statistics
• Works 100% offline — no account required
• Short video ads appear occasionally between games
