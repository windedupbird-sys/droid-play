What's new in Color Tap v1.3.0:
• The game now pauses automatically when you leave the app — no more losing a run to a phone call or a notification
• Fixed the timer and target lifetimes drifting if the device clock changed
• Ads now respect a steadier rhythm and no longer interrupt at random
• The back button behaves as expected: pause during a run, leave the menu otherwise
• Crisper, redrawn interface icons that look the same on every device
• Bigger touch targets and screen-reader labels on every button
• Targets scale up properly on tablets and large screens
• Score pop-ups are now readable on every target colour
• Fixes a bug where the ad counter advanced when rotating the device
• Performance: less battery drain while playing, fewer stutters
• Offline play fully supported
